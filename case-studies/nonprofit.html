A ground-up website built to fundraise, tell a mission-driven story, and connect two underserved populations — simultaneously accessible and emotionally resonant.

Challenge

A mission without a platform.

A food equity nonprofit was doing critical work connecting underserved communities to food resources — but their digital presence didn't reflect the weight of their mission. They needed a website that could fundraise, tell a compelling story, and serve two very different user populations at once.

🌐
No website to represent the mission or attract donors
📢
Brand story was fragmented across materials
👥
Two audiences — donors and recipients — with different needs
💚
Content needed to feel warm and credible, not clinical
Process

Story first. Structure second.

Rather than jumping straight into wireframes, I started with the narrative — what story does this organization need to tell, and to whom? Only then did I build the information architecture around the story.

01

Audience definition

Mapped the two primary audiences — donors and community recipients — identifying their motivations, barriers, and the specific moments where the website could move them to act.

02

Content strategy & narrative

Developed a content hierarchy that leads with impact, builds trust through storytelling, and ends with clear calls to action for both audience types.

03

UX design & wireframes

Designed wireframes in Figma from the sitemap outward — homepage, about, programs, donate, and impact pages — each structured to serve both audiences without compromise.

04

Visual design & accessibility

Applied a warm, approachable visual system with WCAG AA-compliant contrast, large readable type, and imagery that centers the community — not poverty.

Website design
Built to fundraise and connect
A warm, credible web presence designed to serve donors and recipients — with clear CTAs, impact storytelling, and accessible design throughout.
The Solution

Warm, credible, and built to convert

The final website tells a cohesive story — leading with impact, grounding it in community, and giving both donors and recipients clear, dignified pathways to engage.

📖

Narrative-led homepage

Opens with the mission in plain, powerful language
Impact numbers surface immediately to build credibility
Testimonials and community imagery create emotional connection
🎯

Dual-audience architecture

Donors: clear "Give" pathway, impact transparency, and trust signals
Recipients: plain-language program descriptions, easy access to resources
Navigation structured so neither audience feels secondary
♿

Accessible & inclusive design

WCAG 2.1 AA contrast throughout — readable by everyone
Large tap targets, clear labels, keyboard navigable
Imagery centers dignity — community members, not hardship
Impact
What the website made possible
A unified, professional digital home that reflects the weight of the mission
Donor and recipient journeys designed with equal care — no audience feels like an afterthought
WCAG-compliant — accessible to the communities the organization actually serves
Content framework that can grow — new programs and stories slot in cleanly
Takeaway

Design as advocacy

This project reinforced something I believe deeply: good design isn't neutral. Every decision — the words, the hierarchy, the imagery — either centers the community or doesn't. Getting it right means the organization can do more of the work that actually matters.
