Design principles
What guides every design decision I make
Hick's Law
Fewer choices, faster decisions
The more options presented, the longer decision-making takes. Simplify navigation and use progressive disclosure to improve completion rates.
Accessibility — WCAG 2.1 AA
Design for everyone, by default
Contrast ratios, keyboard navigation, screen reader support, and clear form labels make products usable for everyone — not as an afterthought.
Cognitive Load
Keep mental effort low
Chunking, familiar patterns, and progressive disclosure reduce mental effort so users can focus on their goal — not on figuring out the interface.
Systems Thinking
You're not the observer. You're a part.
You're inside the system you're designing. Your team, deadlines, and bias all shape what ships. A lot of "user problems" are actually team problems in disguise.

About me

Hi — I'm Christine Arowolaju, a product designer who believes great design is invisible. When it works, people don't notice the design — they just feel capable, confident, and at ease.

I specialise in designing screens and systems that make complex experiences feel simple — from enterprise design systems to mission-driven nonprofit sites to consumer apps for underserved communities.

I think in systems but design for humans. I bring clarity to messy problems, care to every interaction, and craft to every pixel.

Outside of design, I care deeply about accessibility, equity in tech, and creating work that actually changes lives — not just satisfies metrics.
