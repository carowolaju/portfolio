JPMorgan Chase · Conversation design

Business owners kept calling for features they already had. So we brought the features to the conversation.

Chase's AI assistant for business banking customers wasn't closing the gap between what owners asked for and where those features lived in the app. In six weeks, I designed 40+ conversations that connect each request directly to the right feature.

01 — Context & challenge

Support calls were telling us where the app broke down.

Business owners were calling customer service, or getting stuck with the AI assistant, to ask for things the Chase app could already do.

For an owner, every call is time away from running the business. For Chase, it's a cost and a sign that features aren't being found.

How might we turn the assistant into the fastest route from a question to the feature that answers it?

03 — The work

40+ intents, four themes.

We sorted every request from the calls and chat logs into intents, then grouped them by what owners were trying to get done.

Moving money

  • "How do I send a wire?"
  • "Set up an ACH payment"
  • "Zelle for my business"

Managing access

  • "Add an employee"
  • "Change what my bookkeeper can see"

Finding documents

  • "Download last month's statement"
  • "Where are my tax forms?"

Account maintenance

  • "Update my business address"
  • "Order checks"
  • "Lock my card"

It wasn't a missing-feature problem. It was a findability problem.

04 — Key design decisions

Two calls that shaped every conversation.

Should the assistant complete tasks in chat, or send owners to the app?

ChosenDeep-link to the existing featureConfirm what they're trying to do, then take them straight to it.
RejectedRebuild the task inside chatSlower to build, and it would move sensitive steps like wires out of secure screens.

The call: it was faster to ship, sensitive steps stayed in the app's secure flows, and every link taught owners where the feature lives.

What happens when the assistant doesn't understand?

ChosenClarify, suggest, then routeAsk a clarifying question, offer the closest options and route to a person when needed.
RejectedA generic "I didn't understand"It leaves owners at a dead end, and they call support anyway.

The call: no dead ends. Every path ends at a feature, an option or a person.
